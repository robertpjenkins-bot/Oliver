Ollie OS Installer for Mac

1. Plug in a USB drive (8 GB or more). Everything on it is erased.
2. Right-click "Ollie OS Installer.command" and choose Open (the first time
   macOS asks, because it is not from the App Store: click Open).
3. Pick the drive, type ERASE, then this Mac's password.
4. When it says Done, start the computer from the USB drive:
   Intel Mac: hold Option while it starts, pick "EFI Boot".
   Steam Machine: hold Volume Down.  Dell and most PCs: tap F12.
