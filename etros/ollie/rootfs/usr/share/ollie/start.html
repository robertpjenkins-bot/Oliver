OLLIE OS

Anything you download is saved to Downloads, which Etros shares. When you go back to Etros, ROMs are moved into the folder for their console and show up in the menu.

  • Only download games you have the right to: homebrew, free releases, and dumps of cartridges you own.
  • Files whose console can't be told from the name (.bin, .iso, .chd, arcade .zip) stay in Downloads. Move them into ROMs yourself from the desktop.

Gamepad: stick or d-pad moves the pointer · A click · Y right-click · B back · X keyboard · right stick scrolls · L/R page up/down · START enter · SELECT escape · hold SELECT+START to go back to Etros.
