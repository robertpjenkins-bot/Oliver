ETROS UPDATER

Brings an Etros card up to date: the newest Etros, Ollie OS's settings and
My ROMs. Your games, saves and settings stay on the card.

1. Put the Etros card in this computer (a card reader is fine).
2. Double-click Etros Updater (on Linux: Etros Updater, then Run; on a Mac:
   right-click, Open; on Windows: Etros Updater.exe).
3. Pick the card from the list.
4. When it says CARD UPDATED, take the card out and put it back in your
   Raspberry Pi 5 or R36S.

If an update fails, Etros Updater tries once more. If it fails again it
says why, and nothing on the card is lost. No password is ever needed.
