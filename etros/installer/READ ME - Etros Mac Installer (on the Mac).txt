ETROS MAC INSTALLER (run on the Intel Mac itself)

Puts Etros and Ollie OS on this Intel Mac in place of Boot Camp's Windows
(or, with no Boot Camp, in room taken from macOS). macOS and your files
stay. For Macs that can't do Target Disk Mode, like the 12-inch MacBook.

1. Unzip this on the Mac, right-click etros-mac-installer and choose Open
   (macOS asks once whether to open a program from the internet).
2. It shows what stays and what is deleted. Type ERASE to go ahead.
   If macOS wants your Mac password to change its drive, type it in the
   window: that's macOS asking, not Etros.
3. It downloads Batocera for PCs (about 3 GB) and Arch Linux, and asks for
   your Wi-Fi password, which Etros needs to make Ollie OS.
4. When it says ETROS IS ON THIS MAC: restart holding Option and choose
   EFI Boot.
5. The first start sets up Etros's storage. Then Etros makes Ollie OS over
   Wi-Fi in the background (30 to 60 minutes) and says when it's ready:
   ETROS -> Boot into Ollie OS.

In Etros, use a game controller: the Mac's own keyboard and trackpad don't
work in Batocera. They do in Ollie OS. Started normally, the Mac is macOS.
