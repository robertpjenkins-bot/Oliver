ETROS MAC INSTALLER (on an Intel Mac)

Puts Etros and Ollie OS on an Intel Mac, from macOS itself (for Macs that
can't do Target Disk Mode, like the 12-inch MacBook).

1. Open Etros Mac Installer.dmg, then right-click Etros Installer and choose
   Open (macOS asks once whether to open a program from the internet).
   It opens as a page in Safari.
2. It lists every drive and partition. Pick where Etros goes:
   - a partition (Boot Camp, or one you made for Etros in Disk Utility):
     everything on it is erased;
   - macOS's free room: macOS and its files stay;
   - a whole USB drive: everything on it is erased, and it then starts
     Etros on any Intel Mac (plug it in, hold Option at start-up).
   The Mac's start-up files and the macOS you're using are never erased.
3. Type ERASE and click Put Etros on it. If macOS wants your Mac password to
   change a drive, it asks in its own window: that's macOS, not Etros.
4. It downloads Batocera for PCs (about 3 GB) and Arch Linux. Your Wi-Fi
   password (on the page) is for Etros, to make Ollie OS.
5. When it says ETROS IS ON ...: restart holding Option and choose EFI Boot.
6. The first start sets up Etros's storage. Then Etros makes Ollie OS over
   Wi-Fi in the background (30 to 60 minutes) and says when it's ready:
   ETROS -> Boot into Ollie OS.

In Etros, use a game controller: the 12-inch MacBook's keyboard and
trackpad don't work in Batocera. They do in Ollie OS. Started normally, the
Mac is macOS as before.

NO WAITING, NO DOWNLOADS ON THE MAC
Before downloading anything, Etros Installer looks everywhere on the Mac
(Downloads, Desktop, Documents, USB drives...) for Batocera for PCs and Arch
Linux. So on the Steam Machine, run Etros Mac Installer, choose 2 (get the
Mac's downloads onto a USB drive), and copy Etros Mac Installer (app).zip
onto that drive too. On the Mac, copy the zip from the USB drive and open
it: files copied from a USB drive aren't checked by macOS, and Etros
Installer finds Batocera and Arch Linux on the drive.

If macOS says "Checking" or "Verifying" for ages: cancel it, open Terminal,
type  xattr -cr  and a space, drag Etros Installer into the window, press
Enter, then open Etros Installer again.
