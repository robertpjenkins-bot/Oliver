ETROS MAC INSTALLER

Puts Etros (and Ollie OS) on an Intel Mac in place of Boot Camp's Windows,
from a Steam Machine, Steam Deck or other Linux computer. macOS and your Mac
files stay. No password is ever needed.

1. Turn the Mac off and connect it to this computer with a USB-C cable.
2. Turn the Mac on holding T until a USB symbol shows (Target Disk Mode).
3. Double-click Etros Mac Installer, then Run.
4. Pick the Mac, check what it will do, and type ERASE.
   Everything in Boot Camp (Windows and its files) is deleted.
5. It downloads Batocera for PCs (about 3 GB) and, for Ollie OS, Arch Linux,
   and makes Ollie OS (30 to 60 minutes; this computer needs internet and
   about 10 GB free).
6. When it says ETROS IS ON THE MAC: hold the Mac's power button until it
   turns off, unplug it, and turn it on holding Option. Choose EFI Boot.

In Etros, use a game controller: the Mac's own keyboard and trackpad don't
work in Batocera. They do in Ollie OS (ETROS -> Boot into Ollie OS).
Turned on normally, the Mac starts macOS as before.
