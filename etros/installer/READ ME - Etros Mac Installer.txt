ETROS MAC INSTALLER

Puts Etros (and Ollie OS) on an Intel Mac, on partitions you choose,
from a Steam Machine, Steam Deck or other Linux computer. macOS and your Mac
files stay. No password is ever needed.

1. Turn the Mac off and connect it to this computer with a USB-C cable.
2. Turn the Mac on holding T until a USB symbol shows (Target Disk Mode).
3. Double-click Etros Mac Installer, then Run.
4. Pick the Mac. It lists the Mac's partitions: type which one is BATOCERA
   and which is SHARE (the one right after it; the same one twice splits
   it in two). macOS's own can't be picked. Then type ERASE.
   The USB-C cable must carry data (Apple's charging cable doesn't).
5. It downloads Batocera for PCs (about 3 GB) and, for Ollie OS, Arch Linux,
   and makes Ollie OS (30 to 60 minutes; this computer needs internet and
   about 10 GB free).
6. When it says ETROS IS ON THE MAC: hold the Mac's power button until it
   turns off, unplug it, and turn it on holding Option. Choose EFI Boot.

In Etros, use a game controller: the Mac's own keyboard and trackpad don't
work in Batocera. They do in Ollie OS (ETROS -> Boot into Ollie OS).
Turned on normally, the Mac starts macOS as before.

NO CABLE GETS THE MAC THROUGH?
After a minute with no Mac, type u: it makes an Etros USB stick (40 GB or
more; it's erased) with Ollie OS. Then:
1. On the Mac, in macOS Disk Utility, name two partitions BATOCERA (8 GB or
   more) and, right after it, SHARE (32 GB or more). Any format.
2. Turn the Mac off, plug in the stick, turn it on holding Option and
   choose EFI Boot. Etros starts from the stick.
3. In Etros: ETROS -> Put Etros on this Mac. Choose it once, then again.
4. When it says it's done: turn off, take the stick out, turn on holding
   Option, choose EFI Boot. That's Etros from the Mac's own drive.

OR: FOLDERS TO COPY IN FINDER (no cable, no starting from a stick)
Open Etros Mac Installer and type f. It makes "Etros for Mac" on a USB
drive (FAT32 or ExFAT, 5 GB free) with two folders, BATOCERA and SHARE.
1. On the Mac, in Disk Utility: BATOCERA is MS-DOS (FAT), 8 GB or more;
   SHARE is the partition right after it, ExFAT, 32 GB or more.
2. Open the BATOCERA folder on the USB drive, select everything inside
   (Command-A) and drag it onto the BATOCERA partition.
3. SHARE's folder is empty on purpose: copy nothing onto SHARE. Etros sets
   it up the first time it starts.
4. Restart holding Option and choose EFI Boot. Etros makes Ollie OS over
   Wi-Fi (30 to 60 minutes) and says when it's ready.
