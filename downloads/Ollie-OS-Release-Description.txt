## 🎮 Ollie OS — The Big Fun Update!

Made by an awesome 8 year old. Great for gaming: comes with Steam, Wine and more, plus a debugger and a driver installer. Easy to install!

### 🆕 What's new

**🕹️ Games**
- **Games app** – all your games in one place: Steam games (with their covers), Minecraft and more
- **Drag and drop game files** – ROMs (NES, SNES, Game Boy, GBA, N64, DS, Sega), ISOs (PlayStation 1 & 2, PSP, GameCube, Wii) and Windows games (.exe). It works out which console each game is for and installs the right player by itself
- **Game Mode** – Steam opens in big-screen Game Mode, like a Steam Machine
- **Speed Boost** – full power while you play 🚀
- **FPS counter** in games (Right Shift + F12 hides it)
- **More controllers** work when you plug them in
- **Minecraft** – the official launcher, one click to get it ⛏️
- **Couch Co-op** – plug in controllers and see who is Player 1, 2, 3 and 4, then pick a game to play together 👫
- **Box art** – game files in the Games app get their cover pictures by themselves
- **Game Overlay** – choose just the FPS, everything (CPU, GPU, temperature) or nothing

**🐾 Fun**
- **Ollie Pet** – your own cube pet! Name it, feed it, play with it and put it to bed 💖 It grows up (baby, kid, then grown-up with a crown 👑), plays 3 mini-games (Catch, Hide and Seek, Dance Party) and can walk along the bottom of your screen
- **Typing Game** – pop falling words by typing them, with levels and a best score ⌨️
- **Ollie Finder** – type to find any app or file at once 🔍
- **Start-up animation** (the Ollie cube spins) and a **start-up sound** 🔔
- **Welcome app** with big buttons for everything
- **Colours** – dark or light mode, in teal, purple or red 🎨
- **Ollie talks!** 🗣️ Ollie says hello, cheers you on in games and reads words out loud (switch it off in Settings)
- **Secret Easter eggs** 🤫 – there are hidden surprises to find! Hint: try typing some names into Ollie Finder… and gamers might know a secret code for the Welcome window ⬆️⬆️⬇️⬇️

**🎨 Make stuff**
- **Sticker Maker** – make stickers with shapes, emoji and words, and stick them on your desktop background
- **Comic Maker** – make comics with 1 to 4 panels, characters and speech bubbles 💬
- **Ollie Animator** – draw a cartoon frame by frame and save it as a GIF 🎞️
- **Sound Board** – 12 big buttons with fun sounds, or record your own with the microphone 🔊

**🧠 Learn stuff**
- **Coding Quest** – give a robot instructions to reach the star, 10 levels 🤖⭐
- **World Explorer** – a spinning globe with 176 countries, their flags, capitals and fun facts, plus a "Find it!" game 🌍
- **Space Explorer** – visit the Sun, all 8 planets, the Moon and Pluto 🪐
- **Spelling Bee** – hear a word and spell it, 8 levels from easy to tricky 🐝

**🔔 Updates**
- **Update Checker** – the first start asks if you want it. Then you get a notification when there's a new Ollie OS update, with an Install button
- **Latest updates** – see a list of every update and install them in one click
- **Update Ollie OS** – one click updates everything

**👪 For grown-ups (in Ollie OS Settings, locked with a parent PIN)**
- **Screen Time** – a daily limit with warnings, then "come back tomorrow" 🌙
- **Inappropriate Typing Scanner** – asked about on the first start. It only notes bad words (even misspelled ones), and the notes open only with the parent PIN
- **Firefox password** – the first start asks if Firefox should have a password

### 💾 How to install
- **Installers** (Windows .exe, Mac .dmg, Linux/SteamOS AppImage) download the official Arch Linux ISO and turn it into Ollie OS right on your computer, then ask if you want to write it to a drive. No GitHub, no sign-in!
- When you install it, it asks if **Windows** should be on the start-up menu too
- Starting from the drive: Steam Machine and Steam Deck, hold Volume Down. Dell: tap F12. Mac: hold Option

### ⬆️ Already have Ollie OS?
